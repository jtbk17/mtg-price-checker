🔔 Price alerts

    Orders

    Sales to TheTCGMarketplace WTB buyers and from our own listings, saved in the inventory sheets. A listing whose quantity drops shows up as “Looks sold?” for you to confirm. Buyer paid adds the copies to Pulled on the row you take them from; dropped off moves them from Pulled to Sold.

    Add cards

    Scan a stack of about 100 into ManaBox's Inbox binder, export it as CSV, and pick the file here. It's added to the inventory sheet behind the next empty slot, so there's nothing to import, copy or paste.

    Sell plan

    What to do with each card: list just under the cheapest matching TheTCGMarketplace copy (1¢ under, or 10¢ at $10+), but never so low that, after TCGMP's 4% commission, it pays less than GOG Cash + 10%, or below the best WTB offer; sell to a WTB buyer if they pay at least 90% of a realistic sale price (the lower of that list price and Card Kingdom's price); sell to GOG if it pays within 15% of what the cheapest listing would bring after the 4% commission.
